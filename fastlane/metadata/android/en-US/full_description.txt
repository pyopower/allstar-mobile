Mobile ASL Client turns your phone into a radio-less AllStarLink station. It connects to YOUR OWN AllStarLink node over IAX — just like the classic iaxRpt PC client — and from there you can link to any of the ~42,000 nodes and hubs in the network, or to EchoLink repeaters, links and conferences, and talk.

FEATURES
• Push-to-talk (hold or toggle) with transmit time-out, RX/TX level meters
• Full node list with search by number, callsign, place or description, and a live "online" indicator
• EchoLink tab: repeaters, links and conferences on the air right now, with busy status
• Separate favorites and recents for AllStar and EchoLink
• Link, monitor-only, unlink and unlink-all with one tap; DTMF keypad with the standard ASL3 commands explained
• See which linked node is talking (with Allmon3 on your node)
• One-command node setup: run a script on your node and scan the QR code it prints
• Runs in the background, optional auto-connect and start at boot
• External PTT: volume-down key and PTT broadcast intents (works with Bluetooth speaker-mics through PTT Bridge)
• English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French and German

REQUIREMENTS
• An amateur radio licence. Transmitting through a node is subject to your country's regulations.
• Access to an AllStarLink node (ASL3 recommended) reachable from the Internet (UDP 4569).

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no analytics. Your voice goes only to the node you configure; node and EchoLink lists are downloaded from the public allstarlink.org and echolink.org services.

Mobile ASL Client is free software (GPL-3.0) and an independent project, not affiliated with AllStarLink, Inc. or EchoLink.
