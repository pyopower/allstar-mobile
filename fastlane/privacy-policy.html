Mobile ASL Client – Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Developer: C31AG (Adán) · Package: ovh.adan.allstar

Summary

Mobile ASL Client has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The developer does not receive, store or share any personal data.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you press push-to-talk. Your voice is sent directly from your phone to the AllStarLink node you configure in the app (your own server). It is not recorded and is never sent to the developer.

Data stored on your phone

Node profiles (server address, IAX user and password, node number, callsign), favorites, recent nodes and settings are stored only on your device. Android backup of app data is disabled. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Network connections

  • Your AllStarLink node (IAX protocol), and optionally its Allmon3 status page to show who is talking.
  • allstarlink.org (public node list and node statistics) and echolink.org (public list of stations on the air). These requests carry no personal data; those services may log your IP address as any website does.

Background operation

While connected to your node, and if you enable "start when the phone is switched on", the app keeps a foreground service with a visible notification so that the radio link keeps working with the screen off.

Children

The app is intended for licensed amateur radio operators and is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions: write to the developer through the contact address on the Google Play listing.

Política de privacidad (español)

Mobile ASL Client no tiene cuentas, ni publicidad, ni analíticas. El desarrollador no recibe, guarda ni comparte ningún dato personal.

Micrófono: solo se usa mientras pulsas el PTT. Tu voz va directamente desde el móvil al nodo AllStarLink que configures (tu propio servidor). No se graba ni se envía nunca al desarrollador.

Datos en el móvil: los perfiles de nodo (servidor, usuario y contraseña IAX, número de nodo, indicativo), favoritos, recientes y ajustes se guardan solo en tu dispositivo, sin copia de seguridad de Android. Al desinstalar la app se borran.

Conexiones: tu nodo AllStarLink (IAX) y, si existe, su Allmon3 para ver quién habla; allstarlink.org (lista y estadísticas públicas de nodos) y echolink.org (lista pública de estaciones). Esas peticiones no llevan datos personales.

Segundo plano: mientras estás conectado, y si activas «Iniciar al encender el móvil», la app mantiene un servicio con notificación visible para que el enlace siga con la pantalla apagada.

Menores: la app está pensada para radioaficionados con licencia y no se dirige a menores.
